# F-13 — The ticket-gate budget is missed once the engine has code

- **Assumption** (WORKFLOW §6 budget, ADR-0019, measured by #22 on a scratch reactor): with three
  gates in parallel, an engine ticket gate (step 1, including `engine/oracle`) runs under 30 s and
  a service ticket gate under 60 s. Step 2 takes about 15 s.
- **How it fails**: measured on the spec-1 code (`bench/RESULTS.md`, "Ticket-gate budget after
  spec 1"):
  - an engine step 1 takes about 110 s with three in parallel;
  - a service step 1 takes 79.5 s alone;
  - step 2 takes 23.5 s.
  The #22 baseline had almost no production code, no corpus, no differential oracle (Saxon) and
  no `@QuarkusTest`.
- **Severity**: minor. Nothing breaks; gates are slower and three writers gain less. The budget is
  a planning number, not a correctness gate.
- **Evidence**: #21 story 33; the verifier hand-back of the spec-1 gate re-run. The logs are not
  kept; the numbers are in `bench/RESULTS.md`.
- **Change made**: none in spec 1. Spec 5 (speed) profiles the gate: where step 1 spends its time
  (Saxon start-up in `engine/oracle`, JaCoCo, surefire on the module path, Quarkus test start-up).
  Then it either cuts the time or re-sets the budget with the owner. Until then, WORKFLOW §6 keeps
  its numbers as targets.
