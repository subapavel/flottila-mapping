# F-11 — checkForIdentical does not see namespace declarations

- **Assumption** (ADR-0018, Verdict): `checkForIdentical()` makes prefixes and namespace declarations match.
- **How it fails**: XMLUnit 2.14.0 `DOMDifferenceEngine.splitAttributes` drops every attribute in the XMLNS
  namespace. Names, prefixes and URIs are compared, but where a namespace is declared, and how often, is not.
- **Severity**: minor
- **Evidence**: #27 design (XMLUnit v2.14.0 source). The test-author's
  `OracleVerdictTest.identicalSeesAPrefixButNotWhereANamespaceIsDeclared` is the witness.
- **Change made**: the verdict also requires equal per-element declarations (`OracleVerdict.declarations`).
  ADR-0018's Verdict bullet is reworded, and so is "differential case" (GLOSSARY lists it under _Avoid_).
