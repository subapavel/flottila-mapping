# F-31: The canvas marks an unreachable box by a dashed border, not by dimming

- **Assumption** (ADR-0013, line 64): "it is kept on save, and the canvas dims it". GLOSSARY.md (Unreachable box) says the same: "dimmed on the canvas".
- **How it fails**: #138 first dimmed the whole card with `opacity`. In the screenshot review, the card's text fell below a readable contrast, and the card's parameters became hard to edit even though they still count. The card now has a dashed border, a muted head and a "not reached" mark on the head's second line. Its body keeps full contrast.
- **Severity**: minor. The box is still marked as unreachable at a glance, and only the visual means changed.
- **Evidence**: #138 (merged abb15d3). See `FunctionEditor.browser.test.tsx` ("an unreachable box, and only it, is titled "Not reached by the result" and keeps the whole card opaque").
- **Change made**: none to ADR-0013 or GLOSSARY.md. Whether to reword "dims it" as "marks it (dashed border, muted head)" is an owner question. It can go in the same ADR-0013 amendment that F-29 and F-30 ask for.
