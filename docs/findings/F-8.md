# F-8 — json-sKema 0.32.0 is too lenient to be the model's parser, and `patternProperties` checks only the first member

- **Assumption** (ADR-0011 §Validation, F-1): json-sKema 0.32.0 validates the mapping model and
  fails closed.
- **How it fails** (observed in #24):
  - Its `patternProperties` keeps only the result of the first map member, so later members go
    unchecked: `inputs: ["a1","a.1"]` validated.
  - Its parser accepts trailing commas, unknown escapes, raw control characters and leading
    zeros, and it decodes `\ude00` as U+00DE.
  - A `$ref` into a `urn:` id of another document throws `URISyntaxException`.
- **Severity**: major (stage 1 would not fail closed)
- **Evidence**: #24, `t/24-model-format` @ 16fe6d3; the implementer's javap and scratch runs.
  The workarounds are covered by `MappingModelSchemaStageTest` and the reader cases in
  `MappingModelLoadTest`.
- **Change made**:
  - `engine/mapping` parses with its own strict reader (`internal.StrictJsonReader`, with a depth
    limit) and hands json-sKema a tree it has already checked.
  - Every map member is validated on its own.
  - Sub-schemas carry the contract's `$defs` instead of a cross-document `urn:` `$ref`.
  - ADR-0011 stage 1 now names this. A json-sKema upgrade must re-run the stage-1 tests without
    the workarounds before any of them is dropped.
