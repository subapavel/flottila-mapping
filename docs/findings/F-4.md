# F-4 — CI also runs on pushes to ticket branches

- **Assumption** (ADR-0017 §CI): `ci.yml` runs on `pull_request` and on `push` to `main`.
- **How it fails**: a ticket branch (`t/**`) has no PR, so with those triggers its CI cannot be
  observed before it merges. The skeleton ticket needs to observe CI as one of its own
  acceptance criteria.
- **Severity**: minor
- **Evidence**: #22, `t/22-build-skeleton` @ dacc94e. CI `backend`, `ui` and `stack` were
  observed green on the branch push.
- **Change made**: `ci.yml` also runs on `push` to `t/**`. ADR-0017 §CI is amended to say so.
  `concurrency` per ref with `cancel-in-progress` keeps it cheap.
