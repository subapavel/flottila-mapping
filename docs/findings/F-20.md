# F-20 — Saxon-HE rounds a terminating quotient too

- **Assumption** (ADR-0005): "A quotient that terminates is exact."
- **How it fails**: Saxon-HE 12.5 gives every quotient the same scale rule, including one that terminates. `1 div 2^30` (exactly `0.000000000931322574615478515625`) comes out as `0.000000000931322575`. The tie `3 div 524288` gives `…437` with half-down rounding.
- **Severity**: minor
- **Evidence**: #72, cases `fn/divide/004` and `ArithmeticFamilyTest`, DifferentialOracleTest against Saxon-HE 12.5.
- **Change made**: ADR-0005 amended. Every quotient takes `max(18, scale(dividend) − scale(divisor) + 18)` fractional digits, with the last digit rounded half-down. A quotient that terminates within that scale is unaffected. Divide keeps its catalogue deviation against PO.
