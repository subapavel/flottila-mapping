# F-5 — the PIT command needs `-am test-compile`, and ticket-gate step 2 takes about 15 s

- **Assumption** (WORKFLOW §6): spec-gate step 1b is
  `./mvnw -B -pl <class-C modules> org.pitest:pitest-maven:mutationCoverage`, and ticket-gate
  step 2 takes about 8 s.
- **How it fails**:
  - Without `-am`, upstream reactor modules do not resolve.
  - Without `test-compile`, a clean tree has no classes to mutate.
  - With `-am`, PIT also runs on modules that have no mutation floor.
  - Step 2 (`-DskipTests verify`) was measured at 15 s.
- **Severity**: minor
- **Evidence**: #22, `t/22-build-skeleton` @ dacc94e; `bench/RESULTS.md` (2026-10-31).
- **Change made**:
  - Step 1b is now `./mvnw -B -pl <class-C modules> -am test-compile org.pitest:pitest-maven:mutationCoverage`.
  - A `pit.skip` property skips `structure`, `structure-import`, `oracle` and `service`.
  - WORKFLOW §6 is updated to the new command and the measured step-2 time.
  - ADR-0019's PIT scope is unchanged.
