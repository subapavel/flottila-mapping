  fresh worktree and records the result.

  - Check 9 had no evidence on the gate commit and stayed NOT_VERIFIED.
  - The same run showed that `ratchet.java check` ran after the spec gate's step 3 `clean`, so the
    coverage and mutation rows were NOT_RUN.
- **Severity**: major
- **Evidence**: #198 (gate verdict, checks 8 and 9), #205.

    development and `-Pui`, into `.logs/gate-9.log`. The gate-reviewer judges that log.
